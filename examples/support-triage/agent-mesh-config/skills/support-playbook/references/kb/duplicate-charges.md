# Duplicate charges and refunds

Keywords: charged twice, double charge, duplicate, refund, card, payment, billing

Policy:

- A confirmed duplicate charge is refunded in full to the original payment
  method. The refund is issued within 2 business days of confirmation and
  usually shows on the statement 5 to 10 business days later, depending on the
  bank.
- To confirm, we need: the date and amount of both charges, and the last four
  digits of the card. Ask only for what the customer has not already given.
- Never ask for the full card number, the card's security code or online
  banking details. If a customer sends them, tell them not to, and that we
  have not stored them.

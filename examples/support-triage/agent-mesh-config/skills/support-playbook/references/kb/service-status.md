# Service status and outages

Keywords: outage, down, 503, 502, unavailable, cannot load, timeout, incident

- Live status and incident updates: https://status.example.com
- During an incident, the status page is updated at least every 30 minutes,
  and customers can subscribe there for email updates.
- Do not give an estimated time to fix. Say that the team is investigating and
  that updates will follow on the status page.
